Starfacet Chess

Chess at your level, with crystal pieces

Coming soon to iPhone and iPad

An opponent at your level

The built-in engine, Caissa, is one of the strongest chess programs in the world, and it is in the app, so you can play anywhere, with no connection. Choose how strongly it plays, from Novice for players just starting out up to World Champion and Full Strength. Or let it play at your own rating, which rises when you win and falls when you lose, so every game is a fair fight.

Play on a real chess clock, Standard, Rapid, Blitz or your own time control, under FIDE or US Chess rules, with the game as the arbiter. Chess960 is there too.

Over 22,000 puzzles from Lichess come near your puzzle rating, by theme or at random. Ask for a hint in a game, then step through it afterward with the engine's analysis.

What's in the game

  • 3D crystal pieces in ten crystals
  • Levels from Novice to World Champion
  • A real chess clock; FIDE or US Chess rules
  • Over 22,000 rated puzzles by theme
  • Hints, review with analysis, opening names
  • Games saved as PGN, to share or post to Lichess
  • Game controllers, and keyboard play on iPad
  • An original soundtrack
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking
