Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Starfacet Chess does not collect any personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It connects to the internet only when you choose Post on Lichess.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your own device so it can remember them: your finished and unfinished games (as PGN files), your rating and puzzle rating, the puzzles you have solved, and your settings. None of it is sent to us or anyone else. Your games and puzzle files are in the app's own folder, which the Files app shows under Starfacet Chess. Deleting the app removes them.

Post on Lichess

If you choose Post on Lichess for a game, the game sends that one game's moves, with its date, result and the sides' names (such as You and Caissa), to lichess.org to make a public page for it, and gives you the link. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent unless you choose to post. Lichess keeps the game under its own privacy policy.

The App Store

If you get Starfacet Chess from the App Store, Apple handles your purchase and downloads under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in your iPhone or iPad settings, Apple may share anonymous diagnostics with us; you can turn this off in Settings.

Children

Starfacet Chess is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
