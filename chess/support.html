Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email starfacetsupport@gmail.com. Say that it's about Starfacet Chess, whether you play on iPhone or iPad, and what happened just before the problem. If it's about a game, attach its PGN file (Your Games, then Share).

How to play

How to Play, on the title menu, explains the rules of chess and every screen. To start a game, choose Play, then Play against Computer (or Play against Human, for two players on one device), pick your color, the computer's level and a time control, and tap Start Game.

Touch: tap a piece, then the square it goes to, or drag it there. The buttons beside the board resign, offer or claim a draw, ask for a hint (if hints are on), flip the board, and open the menu.

Controller: the D-pad or left stick moves the highlight, or the cursor on the board; A chooses, or picks up and puts down a piece; B or Menu goes back; X flips the board; Y asks for a hint; the bumpers step through the moves in Review.

Keyboard (iPad): the arrow keys move the highlight or the board cursor, Return or Space chooses, Esc goes back, Tab moves between the board and the buttons, F flips the board, H asks for a hint and M turns the music on or off.

Common questions

The computer is too strong (or too easy). Choose another level when you start a game, or Your Rating: the computer then plays at your own rating, which goes up when you win and down when you lose, so it follows you as you improve.

How do puzzles work? Each puzzle is picked near your puzzle rating. Solve it at the first try and your rating goes up; miss it and it goes down. Choose a theme, such as mate in 2 or forks, or Any Theme. You can add your own puzzles as PGN files, in the Files app under Starfacet Chess > Puzzles.

Where are my games saved? On your device. Every finished game is saved as a PGN file, the standard file for chess games: Play > Your Games replays them with the engine's analysis and shares them, and they are also in the Files app under Starfacet Chess > Games. A game you leave with Save and Leave is kept to finish later with Resume a Saved Game. Deleting the app deletes them, so share any you want to keep.

What does Post on Lichess do? It sends that one game to lichess.org, the free chess site, which makes a public web page for it where anyone with the link can replay it. The link is copied for you to share. Nothing is posted unless you choose to.

Which rules does it follow? FIDE's Laws of Chess, or US Chess rules (Settings > Chess Rules). The game acts as the arbiter: it ends the game for checkmate, stalemate, time and insufficient material, and lets you claim a draw by repetition or the fifty-move rule.

Can I turn off the music or hints? Yes: Settings > Sound for the music and sounds, Settings > Chess Engine for hints.
