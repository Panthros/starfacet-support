Starfacet

Falling-crystal space puzzle

Coming soon to iPhone and iPad

Free to try. One in-app purchase unlocks the full voyage.

A voyage, not just a high score

Triplets of faceted crystals drop into a narrow gravity well. Drag to slide them, tap to cycle their order, and flick down to drop. Line up three or more of a kind, across, down or diagonally, to clear them. Crystals above fall into the gaps, and every new match sets off a chain that multiplies your score.

Clear 50 crystals to reach the next sector. The fall speeds up, new crystal colors join as your score climbs, and every clear is worth more.

As you play, a destination comes closer: a planet drawn fresh for every trip, orbiting its own star. Keep going to sector 20, where a blue giant or a black hole waits.

What's in the game

  • Touch controls, with tips while you learn
  • Portrait or landscape, on iPhone and iPad
  • Game controller support, and keyboard play on iPad
  • 16 kinds of planet, seven kinds of star
  • 35 achievements in the Captain's Log
  • An original space music soundtrack
  • No ads, no accounts, no tracking
