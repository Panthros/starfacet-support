Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Starfacet does not collect any personal data. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything over the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things on your own device so it can remember them between games: your Top Pilots scores and the names you type for them, your achievements, the planets and stars you've visited, and your settings (sound, haptics, controls). None of it is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the game, or its save folder, removes it.

Steam and the App Store

If you get Starfacet from Steam or the App Store, Valve or Apple handle your purchase and downloads under their own privacy policies. If the Steam version uses Steam features such as achievements, cloud saves or leaderboards, that information is stored by Steam under Valve's privacy policy. If you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in your iPhone or iPad settings, Apple may share anonymous diagnostics with us; you can turn this off in Settings.

Children

Starfacet is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
